DropMp3

A simple drag-and-drop music player.

Information

Created2026-06-14
Build date2026-06-14
AuthorMVCreator / Codex
PurposeLocal audio playback, playlist management, and subtitle checks

Features

  • Plays MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC, WMA, and other supported audio files.
  • Accepts audio files, folders, and M3U8/WPL playlists by drag and drop.
  • Loads saved playlists from the _conf folder.
  • Supports playlist search, shuffle, filename sort, and repeat playback.
  • Shows album art, random playlist artwork, and one-shot playback.
  • Supports SRT display, subtitle checks, and Whisper-based subtitle creation.

Settings are stored in _conf, Help files in _conf\html, and saved playlists in _conf\lst.
